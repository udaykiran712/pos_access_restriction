Why Do Retailers Need This Module?

Standard Odoo POS creates checkout bottlenecks and allows accidental negative inventory sales.

Standard Odoo POS

  • • Cashiers must open the (i) info popup just to check stock.
  • • Negative inventory is permitted by default.
  • • Unit price is not visible on the product card tile.
  • • Cashiers can over-sell stock easily.

With This Extension

  • • Instant Price Badge: View live selling prices right on the tile.
  • • Location Stock Status: Color-coded stock count (Green/Orange/Red).
  • • Strict Sale Blocker: Halts checkout when stock is 0.
  • • Location-Scoped: Strictly computes stock for the POS warehouse.

Three Powerhouse Features

1. Real-Time Price Tag

Displays a top-left price badge on every product card. Fully synchronized with customer pricelists, tax configurations, and session currency.

2. Physical Stock Badge

Displays a bottom-right badge showing live physical stock in the active POS location:

In Stock (> 5 Units)
Low Stock (1 – 5 Units)
Out of Stock (≤ 0 Units)

3. Strict Sale Blocker

A unified protection barrier. Blocks product card taps, barcode scans, and search selections when stock is zero or insufficient.
