POS Product Stock & Price Display

Give every cashier live, location-accurate pricing and stock visibility right on the product tile - and make it physically impossible to sell what isn't on the shelf.

⚡ Zero N+1 Queries 🎯 Native OWL 2 🔒 LGPL-3

Why This Module Pays for Itself

Three tightly-integrated features that remove guesswork at checkout and protect your inventory from overselling.

⏱️

Faster Checkout

Cashiers see the exact sales price on every tile instantly - no tapping into a product to confirm pricing, no register slowdowns during rush hours.

📉

Zero Negative Inventory

Stock is validated against the exact POS source location before a line is ever created, eliminating the negative-stock cleanup that eats into your inventory team's week.

🛡️

Error Prevention at the Source

A single, native blocking dialog - triggered from tile taps, barcode scans, and search alike - stops out-of-stock sales before they happen, not after a reconciliation report.

Three Features. One Seamless Register Experience.

Every feature is built as a non-destructive extension of core Odoo 18 POS components - no core files touched, no upgrade risk.

1

Live Price on Product Tile

A sleek, translucent price pill renders in the top-left corner of every Product Card, computed live through the POS store so it always reflects the active pricelist, applicable taxes (included or excluded per your POS configuration), and the session's currency formatting - identical to what the customer is actually charged at payment.

Built with non-destructive OWL 2 template inheritance (t-inherit-mode="extension") and a lightweight component patch, so it stacks cleanly with any other module extending the same card.

2

Accurate Physical Location Stock

A second badge shows real, on-shelf quantity at the exact source stock location configured on your POS's operation type - not a generic, company-wide total, and not a figure distorted by virtual or inventory-adjustment locations.

In Stock > 5 Low Stock 1-5 Out of Stock ≤ 0

Consumables and services are automatically excluded - only genuinely stock-tracked products display a badge.

3

Strict Zero & Insufficient Stock Sale Blocker

Every path a product can enter the cart - tapping a tile, scanning a barcode, or selecting from search - funnels through a single guarded entry point. If stock is exhausted or the requested quantity would exceed what's on the shelf, a native confirmation dialog stops the line from being created, with a clear message stating exactly how many units remain.

Refunds and returns are always exempt, so processing a customer return on a zero-stock product is never blocked.

Built for Performance at Scale

Designed and diagnosed against a live Odoo 18 database - not just unit tests.

O(1)

Single Batched Read per POS Load

Stock is resolved via one stock.quant._read_group call for the entire loaded product catalog - not a per-product loop.

0 N+1

Zero N+1 Query Pattern

Both price and stock computation are architected to scale flatly regardless of catalog size.

OWL 2

Native Component Extension

Uses patch() and t-inherit-mode="extension" exclusively - no core template overrides, no monkey-patched core files.

Internal

Physical-Location-Only Accuracy

Stock queries are scoped to location_id.usage = 'internal', so virtual and inventory-adjustment locations never distort the figure shown at the register.
